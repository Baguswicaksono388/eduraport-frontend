# EduRaport Pricing & Subscription Plans

EduRaport utilizes an adaptive cloud SaaS subscription model designed for Indonesian private schools and multi-unit foundations (Yayasan).

## Official Pricing Tiers

### 1. Paket Starter (TK / SD Kecil)
- **Price**: Rp 500.000 / month
- **Scale**: Up to 150 active students per school unit
- **Key Features**:
  - E-Raport TK & SD (PDF generation & download)
  - Visual Template Builder (drag-and-drop narrative design for PAUD)
  - Gradebook engine dasar
  - Student data management
  - Parent Portal (read-only grades & attendance)
  - Basic SPP management
  - WhatsApp support

### 2. Paket Sekolah Menengah (Popular)
- **Price**: Rp 1.200.000 / month
- **Scale**: Up to 500 active students per school unit
- **Key Features**:
  - All features in Starter tier
  - E-Raport SMP & SMA / SMK
  - Weighted Gradebook Engine & automated narrative generation
  - Class scheduling engine with conflict detection
  - Online PPDB (New Student Admission) system
  - Leave permissions & substitute teacher management
  - Integrated School Finance & BOS tracking
  - Automated WhatsApp Gateway notifications

### 3. Paket Enterprise (Yayasan Multi-Unit)
- **Price**: Custom pricing (Tailored to number of school branches & student headcount)
- **Target**: Multi-unit educational foundations (TK + SD + SMP + SMA) and Islamic Boarding Schools (Pesantren)
- **Key Features**:
  - Consolidated Foundation Workspace (cross-branch macro reporting)
  - Unified student identity (TK to SMA without re-entry)
  - Executive Helicopter View WhatsApp digest at 06:00 AM daily
  - Tahfidz Quran progress tracking & Mutaaba'ah Yaumiyah
  - SHA-256 digital signature report verification
  - AI Asisten Guru with PPA 2025 hard guardrails
  - Dedicated Customer Success & SLA support

### 4. EduRaport Solo (B2C Teacher Edition)
- **Price**: Rp 29.000 / month
- **Target**: Individual teachers, private tutors, and home Quran instructors
- **Key Features**: Personal AI Assistant for RPP generation, private lesson tracking, and custom report creation.

## Special Offers & Guarantees
- **Free Pilot Program**: 3-month free pilot program available for TK/PAUD institutions.
- **No Physical Hardware Needed**: 100% cloud-based SaaS. No on-premise servers or dedicated IT team required.
- **Onboarding Speed**: Average full setup and staff training completed within 7 business days.
- **Demo Booking**: 1-on-1 walkthrough demos available on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
